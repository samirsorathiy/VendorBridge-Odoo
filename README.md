# VendorBridge-Odoo

## Odoo x KSV Hackathon 2026

VendorBridge is a Vendor and Procurement Management System designed to simplify the complete procurement lifecycle. The platform helps organizations manage vendors, create RFQs, compare quotations, approve requests, generate purchase orders, manage invoices, and track procurement activities through a centralized dashboard.

---

## Problem Statement

Organizations often face challenges in managing procurement activities due to manual processes, lack of transparency, delayed approvals, and inefficient vendor communication.

VendorBridge provides a digital solution to automate and streamline procurement operations from vendor onboarding to invoice generation.
